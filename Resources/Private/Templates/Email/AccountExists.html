{title} Sent instead of the double-opt-in mail when the submitted address already has an account or a pending request. The signup form deliberately gives no on-screen hint in that case, so this mail is the only place the fact surfaces — and it goes to the address itself, which is the one recipient entitled to know. Deliberately vague: no creation date, no stored data, no user name. Someone who typed the address by mistake learns nothing about its owner. `loginPageUid` comes from the finisher's `variables` option. Without it the mail still makes sense, just without a link.
